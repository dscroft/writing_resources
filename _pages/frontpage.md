As I supervise a number of student projects every year, I have created these pages to offload some of the basic information that I find myself repeating year after year.

This is a collection of resources that I have found useful for my supervisees, and for myself. It is not intended to be a comprehensive guide to writing a thesis or dissertation, but rather a collection of tips and resources that I have found helpful.

*Disclaimer: This is a work in progress. It is not complete and may contain errors.*

*It also contains a lot of my personal opinion and is focused on the sort of student projects that I prefer to supervise.*
*It is not intended to be a definitive guide to writing a thesis or dissertation for all students/supervisors.*
